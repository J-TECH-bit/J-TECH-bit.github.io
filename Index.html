Faith, calling and service

My faith shapes the purpose behind my work.

I am a Christian committed to the mission of God and to serving people with integrity, humility and excellence. My interest in missions includes understanding communities, supporting mission mobilisation and helping make reliable information available for thoughtful, responsible engagement.

I believe knowledge should not end with information alone. It should help us understand life more deeply and respond with wisdom, compassion and goodness before God our Maker.

My commitment: to keep learning, serve faithfully and use the abilities entrusted to me for the good of people and the purposes of God.

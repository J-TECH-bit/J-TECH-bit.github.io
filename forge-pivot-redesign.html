About Joseph

Curious about people.
Committed to useful evidence.

My work brings together research, monitoring and evaluation, data analysis, community engagement and missions mobilisation. I care about asking better questions, listening carefully to communities, and turning findings into decisions that can make a practical difference.

With a background in Science Laboratory Technology and cross-cultural missions training, I continue to develop my skills in data science, digital tools and agricultural innovation.
